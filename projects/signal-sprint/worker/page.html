One shared prompt. One minute. Take turns naming words that fit before the signal moves on. Play together from anywhere.

How to play

  1. Create a room and share its link or code with friends.
  2. On your turn, type a word that starts with the shown letter and fits the category.
  3. Submit before the 60 second timer ends. No repeats.
  4. Earn 1 point for a valid word. First to 5 points wins!
